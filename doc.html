🔒 Most online privacy tools aren't actually private. Every time you upload media to a watermarking site or face-blurring tool, you're handing your files to a stranger's server. For journalists, lawyers, healthcare workers, and content creators — that's a real exposure most people never stop to think about. So I built ObscuraX to solve it. ObscuraX is a professional-grade watermarking and face-redaction studio that runs entirely in your browser — no sign-ups, no server uploads, zero data transmission. Here's what it can do: ✅ Watermark images & videos with text, logos, or links ✅ Auto-detect and blur faces using on-device AI ✅ Draw custom privacy masks over any region ✅ Apply cinematic filters — grayscale, vintage, contrast & more ✅ Bulk export up to 20 images as a single ZIP ✅ Full video support with WebM export Under the hood, face detection runs on a professional-grade SSD Mobilenet V1 model — a more robust and accurate CNN that handles group photos and small faces with high precision via face-api.js on a TensorFlow.js backend. The AI processes everything locally with no network calls after the initial model load. Built with React 18 + TypeScript + Vite. All rendering via the HTML5 Canvas API, video capture via MediaRecorder, and bulk exports via JSZip. Clean, fast, and fully open-source. 🎯 Try the live demo → https://obscuraxapp.netlify.app/ No sign-up. No server. No tracking. MIT licensed.
